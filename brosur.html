About dari.

Dari is an iOS navigation app that helps wheelchair users independently explore recreational areas through accessibility aware route guidance and community-updated information about barriers along the way.

Features

  • Accessibility-Aware Navigation
  • Community-Powered Barrier Reporting
  • Accessibility Information
